# Taking your private key

**Acknowledgement version 2026-09-1 — this is the exact text whose hash is
recorded on the chain when you confirm.**

Read this once. It is short because every sentence in it is load-bearing.

## There is no seed phrase

Your account's key was never written down anywhere, as words or otherwise. It
was created in three pieces, on three different machines, and it has never
existed in one piece — not on your phone, not on our servers, not in a vault.
That is what has been protecting it.

So we cannot "give you your seed phrase". There isn't one. What you are asking
for is for those pieces to be put together, for the first time, into a private
key.

**What you will receive is a private key**, as a long string of characters. You
can import it into an ordinary wallet. Do not expect twenty-four words, and do
not accept twenty-four words from anyone claiming to be us — a phrase like that
would lead to a different, empty account.

## What you lose, permanently

Today, moving your money needs two of the three pieces. Nobody — not you alone,
not us, not somebody who steals your phone, not somebody who compromises our
servers — can move it with one. That is the arrangement you are ending.

From the moment your key is shown to you:

- **Anyone who sees it owns your account.** A photograph, a screenshot, a
  cloud-synced note, a support agent asking you to paste it, malware on the
  machine you open it on. There is no second factor behind it any more.
- **A stolen key cannot be cancelled.** We can re-key your account, but
  re-keying deliberately keeps the same address, so the key you were given keeps
  working for ever. There is no revocation. The only escape is to move
  everything to a new account.
- **We can no longer help you.** Not recovery if you lose it, not a freeze if
  somebody drains it, not a reversal. Those were never favours; they were
  properties of the three-piece arrangement, and they end with it.
- **The account is marked, publicly and permanently.** Anyone can see that this
  account's key has been disclosed. Some counterparties may decline to deal with
  it.

## What we stop being responsible for

Once you confirm, our obligations as custodian of this account's key end. Any
loss after that point — theft, malware, a mistaken transfer, a key you
misplace — is yours. We are not declining to help out of unwillingness; after
this we have nothing left to help with, because we no longer hold anything that
stands between your money and whoever has that key.

Your funds remain yours, and everything else about the platform continues to
work normally.

## What happens next

1. You ask. A notice goes to every device enrolled on this account.
2. **You wait.** The waiting period is set by the chain and we cannot shorten
   it, for you or for anyone. It exists so that if this request was not made by
   you, you have time to find out and cancel it.
3. You confirm. You will be shown this page again, because you are allowed to
   change your mind and because the terms must be in front of you at the moment
   you accept them.
4. Your key is assembled on your own device and shown to you once.

You can cancel at any point before step 3, and nothing will have changed.

## If you are not sure

Then do not do this. Nothing is expiring and there is no penalty for waiting.
The arrangement you have now is the one that protects you from your own worst
day, and people most often want their key on the day they are most annoyed with
us — which is exactly the day to sleep on it.
