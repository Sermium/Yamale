What you are pasting

The wallet prints a placement request when a holder asks to be onboarded. It names the account, the country, and the institution the holder says onboarded them. It is a document, not a transaction: it carries no signature and moves nothing.

Four things can stop the recording, and this page checks all four before anything is signed. They are listed under What stops a recording.

One more thing is a warning rather than a refusal. A request that names no institution at all is still recordable, because the chain does not require the document to name you. It is worth reading twice: check the request is yours to act on before you sign.

Read-only, on purpose. Recording where an account belongs is signed by your own group's key. This page holds none, composes no transaction, and sends nothing: it checks the request against the chain and prints the command, so what would be signed is visible before anything asks for a signature.

Why you and not the holder. The first recording of a country is accepted only from the approved participant that onboarded the account, because that is the party that performed the checks and therefore the only one that knows the answer. An account free to name its own perimeter would name the one with no authority watching it.
