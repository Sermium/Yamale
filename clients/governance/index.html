Emergency powers

Frozen wallets

One validator can freeze in a single block. It is fast on purpose — stopping money moving is reversible, so it is worth being wrong about.

Asset recovery cases

Taking somebody's funds is not. These need a supermajority of voting power and, where required, evidence — and once executed they cannot be undone.
