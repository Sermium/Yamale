A walk through the whole system

Every mechanism, and what it refuses to do.

Yamale is a chain for public money: institutional payments, a land register, closed-end vehicles over real assets, and enforcement powers that are bounded by numbers anybody can read. What follows is each mechanism in turn — one sentence on what it does, then the thing it refuses, then a query against the chain running right now, then where to click to see it working.

The refusal is the headline rather than the feature. A ministry is not asking whether this is clever; it is asking whether it can be trusted with a citizen's money and whether it constrains whoever operates it. Every number on this page is read live and carries the height it was read at. Where a proof cannot be obtained the page says so and shows nothing, because nought and could not ask are opposite facts.
