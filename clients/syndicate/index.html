Reading the chain…

Demonstrator. The deals shown here are on a demonstration network. Everything on this page is read from the chain over its public ABCI interface; nothing is simulated in the browser, so an empty platform reads as an empty platform rather than being filled in with an example.

Read-only, on purpose. Committing to a deal moves money. This console holds no key and composes no transaction; each action shows the command that would send it and the account whose signature the chain checks, so what would be signed is visible before anything asks for a signature.

What the chain does and does not decide. It holds the escrow, refuses a close below the minimum, refunds in full when a raise fails, and pays every distribution to whoever holds the token at that moment — including a buyer from a private sale the platform was never told about. It does not perform the KYC checks and does not claim to: it records that a named, accountable operator says they were performed, dated and attributable.
