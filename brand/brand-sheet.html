Yamale · Brand sheet

The Monolith mark, its variants, and the rules for using them.

The idea

A solid block with the Y cut clean out of it, the stem re-filled in brass. The block is the ledger — closed, permissioned, immovable. The cut is the transfer passing through it: two counterparties in, one settled position out. Everything is machined at 45°, with flat terminals and mitred joins, so the mark reads as something milled rather than drawn. That is the whole argument for the institutional register — it should look like it was already on the document.

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space is one quarter of the block height on all four sides — nothing sets inside it, including type, rules and the edge of the page. Minimum sizes: mark 16 px on screen or 6 mm in print; horizontal lockup 120 px or 30 mm. Below 32 px, switch to the small-size cut.

Wordmark

Drawn, not set: six letters built entirely from straight strokes on the same 45° logic as the mark. Cap height 20 units, stroke 3.2, side bearing 1, tracking 7 — that is 0.35 em, deliberately open so it holds at small sizes and reads as a mark rather than a word. There is no font dependency; the letters are paths. For supporting type, set ISO-style material in a neutral grotesk (Inter, Söhne or Helvetica Neue) and never re-set the wordmark itself.

Do & don't

Do

  • Use the supplied files. Every asset here is drawn from one set of coordinates.
  • Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the block height on all four sides.
  • Put the brass stem against navy or white only — it is the one warm note in the system.
  • Drop to the small-size cut at 32 px and below.
  • Lead with the horizontal lockup; use the mark alone once context is established.

Don't

  • Do not rotate, skew, stretch or re-proportion the block.
  • Do not recolour the stem, or fill it with the same ink as the arms.
  • Do not add shadows, glows, bevels, strokes or gradients to the mark.
  • Do not place the navy block on a navy or Deep Ink field — switch to the dark-background mark.
  • Do not redraw the Y at a different weight, or set the wordmark in a substitute typeface.
  • Do not use the soft cut and the primary cut in the same piece.
